## Figure 1

Retrieval workflow and measurement provenance. Candidate documents, selected fragments, actual context C, answers and extracted claims are different content objects. Dashed links indicate reuse or record association rather than causal effects. Every query–condition position requires its eligible evaluation fields despite canonical content reuse. Fidelity concerns answer-to-claim meaning; support concerns actual context-to-claim entailment; coverage concerns required aspects.

## Figure 2

Withdrawal of an apparent selection advantage. Panel a shows all 175 frozen query-level Selection-minus-Hybrid a3 differences before and after repair, including 54 identical-context and 121 differing-context queries. Panel b displays stored means of these same observations. Panel c identifies 38/700 corrected positions, 37 Selection and one Hybrid. Historical raw p=.0006 belongs to the withdrawn result; corrected post hoc p=.6471 does not establish equivalence. All 175 observations are retained; coincident coordinates overlap without jitter. Lines connect means from two record versions, not independent experiments. In panel c, the asterisk marks the missing-to-one fallback, which applies only to supported aspects with absent completion.

Source: Author’s figure based on the frozen study records.
